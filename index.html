Controls

On foot

  • W / ↑ walk forward · S / ↓ step back · A D / ← → turn · hold Shift sprint
  • F jab · G heavy kick · Space dodge roll (only in fights; punching civilians or police gets you wanted)
  • E interact: missions, doors, bartender, social wall, the scenic viewer at the end of Sunset Pier (A D switch views) · get into a car
  • Hold E next to a parked car to steal it (witnesses may call the police)

Driving

  • W / ↑ accelerate · S / ↓ brake & reverse · A D / ← → steer
  • Space handbrake (drift) · E get out (slow down first)

Police

  • Stars show your wanted level. Break line of sight and get far from where you were last seen (the blue circle on the radar) until the search ends.
  • Standing still next to a police car, or an officer reaching you, means BUSTED (a fine).

General

  • M or Esc pause, map & missions · V first / third person camera · N day / night
  • R respawn nearby if you are ever stuck · drag the mouse to look around

How it works

Each district of the city holds one part of my portfolio. Drive into a glowing yellow marker to start that district's mission. Finishing it unlocks the section in the Dossier. Blue tokens scattered around the city raise your respect.

Built with Three.js, Rapier physics and Vite. Progress is saved in your browser.
